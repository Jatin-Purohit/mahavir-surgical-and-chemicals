About Us

Built Around Institutional Requirements

Mahavir Surgical and Chemicals was established in 2018 and is engaged in supplying high-quality laboratory, scientific, educational, electronics, and sports equipment to schools, colleges, universities, government institutions, and other organizations across India.

The company focuses on providing reliable products, competitive pricing, responsive service, and dependable supply support for institutional and government requirements.

Our business combines traditional laboratory and scientific supplies with modern technology and electronics products, enabling us to serve the evolving requirements of educational and technical institutions.
